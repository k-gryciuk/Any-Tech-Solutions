# Any Tech Solutions

PART 1: CONTENT

1. What is the name of your business?
Any Tech Solutions

2. Is the business real or fictional?
Real

3. What products or services does the business offer?
Tech repair, mobile repair, networking, printer repair, and more.

4. Who is the target audience?
Older generations who are less technologically inclined.

5. What problem does this business solve?
It fixes broken devices, networking and Wi-Fi issues, and printer problems.

6. What makes this business unique compared to competitors?
We provide in-home services.

7. What pages will your website include?
Home, About, Services, and Contact.

8. Write a short mission statement for the business.
At Any Tech Solutions, we provide tech repair services for anyone who needs them. Unlike our competitors, we come to you. You can call us 24/7, and we can have someone at your door within 20 minutes.

9. What tone should the website content have?
Plain, professional, and friendly.

10. What call-to-action do you want visitors to take?
Call us 24/7 for anything you need help with.

11. What information must be immediately visible on the homepage?
The services we provide.

12. Will the site include images, videos, or other media? Why?
Yes. The site will include images of our services, videos of repairs, and before-and-after photos. These show customers the quality of our work and build trust.

PART 2: DESIGN

1. What overall style will the website have?
Minimalist.

2. What color scheme will you use and why?
A grayish blue. It looks calm and professional, and it helps visitors feel comfortable and trusting.

3. What fonts will you use for headings and body text?
Arial for both headings and body text.

4. How will spacing and layout improve readability?
Generous padding between sections allows each area to stand on its own and makes the page easy to read.

5. What visual hierarchy will guide the user's attention?
Content will appear in this order: website name, introduction, and then services. The services will be shown in a bullet-point style.

6. How will consistency be maintained across pages?
Every page will use the same sizes, fonts, and colors.

7. What icons will you use?
A website icon.

8. What emotions should the design evoke in users?
Relief, trust, and confidence.

9. How will accessibility be considered?
We will use large font sizes for the company name and the services, with the services slightly smaller than the company name. We will also use high contrast between text and background.

10. What inspiration sources influenced your design?
Geek Squad.

11. Describe the layout of the homepage and build the wireframe.
The homepage has the business name, "Any Tech Solutions," at the top. Below it is a navigation bar with four buttons: Home, Services, Contact, and About. Under the navigation bar are a short introduction and the list of services.

PART 3: INTERACTIVITY

1. What interactive elements will your site include?
Forms, menus, and buttons.

2. How will users navigate the website?
Through buttons for each page.

3. Will there be form validation or user feedback? Explain.
Yes. When a user books an appointment, the form will check that all required fields are filled in correctly and will show a confirmation or error message.

4. What JavaScript functionality will you implement?
Form validation and a mobile menu toggle.

5. How does interactivity improve user experience?
It makes the site faster and clearer to use.
